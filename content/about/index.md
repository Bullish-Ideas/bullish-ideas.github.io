Bullish Ideas is a home for early concepts, experiments, and writing about design and technology.

Some pieces begin with a question. Others examine an interaction, a design principle, or a way to explain a technical idea.

Each piece states its premise and shares enough detail to make the idea useful. Concepts remain ideas until someone tests or builds them.
